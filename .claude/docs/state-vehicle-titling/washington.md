# Washington — Titling an Imported Vehicle

State-specific procedures for titling and registering a vehicle imported into Washington from outside the United States, with focus on Canadian imports.

**Authority**: RCW Title 46 (Motor Vehicles), WAC Title 308 (DOL regulations); RCW 82.12 (Use Tax). Administered by the **Washington Department of Licensing (DOL)**.

## Documents to Bring to the WA DOL Title Appointment

| Document | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **CBP-stamped HS-7** (DOT Declaration) | Border | Original. Without it WA DOL will not title. |
| **CBP-stamped EPA 3520-1** | Border | Original. |
| **CBP Form 7501** (Entry Summary) | Border | Original; serves as proof of customs clearance and declared value. |
| **Foreign title** = Alberta Vehicle Registration Certificate | Seller | CBSA-stamped/surrendered. WA accepts this as the foreign title for titling purposes per RCW 46.12.560. |
| **Notarized Bill of Sale** (English, with USD value) | Buyer + seller | Must show purchase price for use-tax calculation. |
| **VIN inspection certificate** | WSP or DOL | See below. Required for any vehicle with no prior US title. |
| **Vehicle Title Application** (TD-420-001) | DOL | Filled out by user; can be done at the appointment. |
| **Odometer disclosure** | Form on TD-420-001 | 1968 vehicles are pre-1979 and federally exempt, but WA still has a state line. Mark "exempt" if applicable. |
| **Proof of WA insurance** (binder card or declarations page) | Insurer | Required at registration; the title alone doesn't require insurance, but you cannot register without it. |
| **Photo ID + proof of WA residency** | User | Driver's licence, utility bill, lease. |
| **Use tax payment** (debit/credit/check) | At DOL | See calculation below. |

## VIN Inspection (Required)

Foreign-titled vehicles must have a **VIN inspection** before WA DOL will issue a title. Authority: RCW 46.12.560(5), WAC 308-56A-105.

**Where**: Washington State Patrol (WSP) inspection stations OR DOL-authorized inspection sites. Find a list at dol.wa.gov.

**What they verify**: VIN on the dash matches VIN on the foreign title, no signs of alteration, vehicle is the one described.

**Fee**: ~USD 50-75 (set by WSP).

**Timing**: Inspection is **valid for 90 days** after issuance. Schedule it after the border crossing but before the DOL title appointment. Some inspection stations are walk-in; some are appointment.

**Tip**: Some buyers schedule the VIN inspection within a day or two of crossing the border, then schedule the DOL title appointment for shortly after. The vehicle is on a trip permit during this window.

## Use Tax — The Largest Cost

**Authority**: RCW 82.12.020 (use tax), RCW 82.08 (sales tax — informs the rate). Use tax mirrors the sales tax rate at the buyer's WA address.

**Rate**: 6.5% state + local (varies by county/city).

| Example WA address | Approximate combined rate (2026) |
|---|---|
| Seattle (King County) | ~10.1% (varies by city — Seattle is at the high end) |
| Bellingham (Whatcom) | ~8.7% |
| Spokane | ~8.9% |
| Vancouver (Clark) | ~8.5% |
| Olympia (Thurston) | ~9.4% |
| Unincorporated rural | ~7.9% (state + county only) |

**Always look up the exact rate** for the buyer's address: dor.wa.gov/find-taxes-rates.

**Taxable basis** (RCW 82.12.010(7)): the **purchase price** OR the **fair market value** (FMV), whichever is greater. WA DOL has discretion to use FMV if the declared price looks low relative to market.

- For collector vehicles, Hagerty Valuation Tool, NADA Classic, and recent comparable auction sales (Bring a Trailer, Mecum) are the de facto FMV references DOL clerks use.
- A purchase price equal to or above NADA "good condition" value is rarely challenged.
- An obviously below-market price (e.g., $5K for a #3-condition '68 Firebird) will be replaced with FMV by the clerk.

**Tax credit for foreign tax paid** (RCW 82.12.035): WA gives a credit for sales/use tax legally imposed by **another US state** OR by certain other jurisdictions. **Alberta has no provincial sales tax** so there's nothing to credit. Some buyers ask about the federal Canadian GST (5%) — WA does **not** credit GST. The full WA use tax is owed.

**Calculation example** — $30,000 USD purchase, Bellingham WA address, 8.7% combined rate:
```
$30,000 × 0.087 = $2,610 use tax
```

**Calculation example** — $30,000 USD purchase, Seattle WA address, 10.1% combined rate:
```
$30,000 × 0.101 = $3,030 use tax
```

## Title and Registration Fees

In addition to use tax (RCW 46.17 and 46.18 schedules):

| Fee | Amount (2026, approx.) |
|---|---|
| Title application | $15 |
| Title fee (electronic processing) | $7-12 |
| License plate fee | $10 (standard) — variable for specialty/personalized |
| Vehicle registration (annual) | $30 (basic) + Regional Transit Authority surcharge if applicable + weight fee |
| RTA tax (King/Pierce/Snohomish) | 1.1% of MSRP-derived value (significant for newer vehicles; minimal for a 1968 — see below) |

For a vehicle ≥10 years old, RTA tax is calculated against a depreciated value floor — minimal impact for a 1968.

## Antique / Collector Vehicle Registration

**Authority**: RCW 46.18.220 (Collector Vehicle plates), RCW 46.16A.220 (Year-of-manufacture plates).

### Collector Vehicle Plates (RCW 46.18.220)

Eligibility: vehicle **30 years or older** AND used "primarily as a collector vehicle." 1968 (58 years old): qualifies.

**Fee**: $35 one-time fee (in addition to standard title/registration). **No annual renewal** of vehicle registration after that — perpetual.

**Use restrictions**: "Public displays, parades, club activities, exhibitions, tours, and similar uses." Plus "incidental other use." This phrase has been litigated — daily commuting is not OK; occasional pleasure-driving and weekend events are. WSP enforcement is light but legal exposure exists for clearly daily-driver use.

**Trade-offs**:
| Standard registration | Collector plate |
|---|---|
| ~$60-100/year recurring | $35 once, never again |
| No use restrictions | "Collector use" only |
| Standard plates | Distinct collector plates |

A buyer who plans to drive the car 5,000+ miles/year as a regular vehicle: choose **standard** registration.
A buyer who'll drive ~1,000 miles/year for shows and weekend cruises: choose **collector**. Pays for itself in 1-2 years and never renews.

### Year-of-Manufacture Plates (RCW 46.16A.220)

Vehicles ≥30 years old can use a **period-correct license plate** matching the vehicle's model year. The buyer sources an actual 1968 Washington license plate (eBay, plate-specific dealers; ~$50-300 depending on condition) and DOL approves it for use after verifying it's not on another vehicle.

Pairs nicely with collector registration. Cosmetic, but desirable for show cars.

## Trip Permit (Critical for Drive-Home Logistics)

**Authority**: RCW 46.16A.320; WAC 308-93-090.

**Validity**: 3 days from issuance.

**Fee**: $30.

**Where to buy**:
- DOL website: dol.wa.gov (print at home)
- Any DOL vehicle licensing office
- DOL-licensed vendors (some auto-parts stores, some title companies near border crossings)

**Critical timing**: The permit must be obtained **before driving on WA roads** with the imported vehicle. The vehicle has no WA registration coming off the border, so without the permit, every mile is illegal.

**Recommendation**: Buy the trip permit online a day or two before crossing the border, print it, fill in the dates carefully so it covers the drive-home window. Some buyers print blank permits and fill in the date when they cross — DOL's instructions specify the dates be filled at issuance, not later, so this practice is risky.

**Multiple permits**: a buyer can get up to **3 trip permits per year per vehicle** (RCW 46.16A.320(2)). The 3-day window is firm; a longer drive home (e.g., over a holiday weekend) may need scheduling around it.

## Insurance Requirement

WA does not require insurance to **title** the vehicle, but requires it to **register** (RCW 46.30.020). Most buyers title and register in the same DOL appointment, so insurance must be in place. See `insurance-team` notes on classic-collector and in-transit binders.

## Emissions and Safety Inspection

WA discontinued routine emissions testing statewide effective 2020-01-01. Not a factor.

WA does not require periodic safety inspections. Federal 25-year FMVSS exemption (`vehicle-import:25-year-rule.md`) means WA cannot require FMVSS modifications anyway.

## Process Order — Recommended Sequence

```
T-7  : Buy trip permit online (print, save)
T-3  : Submit CERS export declaration
T-0  : Cross border, CBP stamp HS-7/EPA/7501
T+0  : Drive home on trip permit (3-day window starts)
T+1-2: Schedule WSP/DOL VIN inspection (some offer same-day walk-in)
T+1-2: VIN inspection done
T+2-3: WA DOL title + registration appointment, pay use tax + fees
       Receive plates same day
```

If the trip-permit window is tight, consider buying a **second** permit before the first expires (allowed up to 3 per year per vehicle).
